Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your session. If something isn’t covered here, reach us at budphotoart@gmail.com.

Will I receive the raw files? +

Raw files are not included. A RAW file is the unprocessed, unfinished image straight from the camera sensor — flat, unretouched, and requiring professional editing software just to view properly. What you receive instead are the completed photographs: carefully color-graded, retouched, and edited to reflect the full creative vision of your session.

Sharing raw files would be like a chef handing you unseasoned, uncooked ingredients instead of the finished dish. The edit is part of the art.

How many photos will I receive? +
Every session includes a minimum of 20 fully edited, high-resolution images. The final count depends on how the session unfolds — the light, the energy, and the natural moments that arise. We never pad a gallery with duplicates, and we never cut it short just to hit a number. Quality over quantity, always.
How does payment work? +

A $100 deposit is due at booking to secure your session date and time. The remaining $100 balance is collected on the day of your shoot.

We accept Venmo and Zelle. Payment details will be shared when you confirm your booking.

Are there any promotions available? +
Returning clients receive 10% off their next session — our way of saying thank you for trusting us with your family’s story again. The discount is applied automatically when you book; just mention that you’ve shot with us before.
Does the $200 fee include tax? +
Yes. The $200 session fee is all-inclusive — no hidden charges, no added taxes at checkout. What you see is what you pay.
