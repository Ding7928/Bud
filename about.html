Who We Are

Cedar Park’s family portrait team

Bud Photography & Art is a portrait photography team based in Cedar Park, TX, dedicated to capturing authentic family moments in the natural beauty of North Austin’s parks and green spaces.

We specialize in outdoor family portrait sessions designed to be relaxed, fun, and stress-free. Whether it’s parents with young kids, multi-generational families, or growing families welcoming a new member, we know how to bring out genuine smiles and real connection.
